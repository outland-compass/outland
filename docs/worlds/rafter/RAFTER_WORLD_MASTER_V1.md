## B. Executive definition
RAFTER is an OUTLAND World built around a compact catamaran on the Danube/Novi Sad target geography. It combines direct contact with the river, self-directed activity, a transformable day/night guest space, lightweight physical-digital interactions and optional Mystery progression into a coherent experience.

RAFTER exists to prove that OUTLAND can create a distinctive World with disciplined capital, not by adding conventional luxury, but by combining place, autonomy, thoughtful physical design and a small amount of technology that genuinely improves the experience.

The product is primarily a **RAFTER river experience / mini-expedition**, potentially including overnight accommodation for a design target of up to eight guests. It is not positioned as a generic floating apartment or classic raft rental.

RAFTER becomes an OUTLAND World through the combination of:
- a specific relationship to place and nature;
- an authored but non-intrusive exploration layer;
- Passport identity and access continuity;
- optional Mystery chapters linked to the physical environment;
- transformable physical design;
- a persistent achievement that survives checkout.

### Shortest realistic operational/game loop
`discover → book → arrive → Passport access → safety onboarding → river/day experience → optional TOK/GLAS/OBALA/NOĆ Mystery → evening space conversion → sleep → morning/check-out → temporary access expires → achievement persists if earned`

## C. Product principles
1. **River first.** Technology and story support the river experience; they do not replace it.
2. **Exploration and autonomy.** The guest should be able to use RAFTER meaningfully without constant staff presence.
3. **Transformable space.** The same compact footprint changes from social/day use to protected night accommodation.
4. **Day openness / night protection.** Inner Room must feel open to the river by day and private, dark, weather-protected and thermally usable at night.
5. **Physical-digital, not digital-first.** Passport, SENSE and Mystery serve concrete guest moments.
6. **Mystery is optional to complete.** It is part of identity but never a prerequisite for a good stay.
7. **Safety is independent.** No NFC, Mystery state, cloud service or game mechanic may control essential safe exit.
8. **Quality without unnecessary luxury.** Robust, warm, tactile, repairable and memorable beats yacht-grade excess.
9. **Modularity.** Furniture, equipment and experience should adapt as real guest evidence emerges.
10. **Value-engineered pilot.** Target €15k; working €15–20k; re-underwrite above €20k.

## E. Spatial layout and circulation
### E1. Orientation
Every plan must explicitly label bow/stern and port/starboard.

### E1A. Main entrance and internal partition
The main physical entrance into the enclosed RAFTER volume is **Front Deck → automatic main entrance → River Room**. During normal guest access, Passport/NFC controls this automatic entrance through a local controller. Exact door/actuator/lock/weather-sealing/power details are `TO VERIFY`. Safe emergency egress must remain mechanically/manual possible independent of Passport/NFC, network/cloud and normal powered automation.

Between River Room and Inner Room is a **lightweight sliding partition** for spatial separation, privacy and day/night transformation. It is not a heavy security/structural door and must preserve the central corridor.

River Room supports dining for 8 using **folding/removable tables and 8 folding chairs**, so dining does not become a permanent spatial layout.

### E2. Boat rack drawing rule
At the outer stern on **both** sides is one vertical rack parallel to the vessel. Each rack stacks:
`SUP top → kayak middle → tandem canoe bottom`.

A top view shows one stack footprint per side, never three boats side-by-side.

### E3. Central circulation
RAFTER uses one continuous central longitudinal corridor: **Front Deck → River Room → Inner Room → Service Core → Dirty Deck/stern**. Passing through the Inner Room is intentional. The service section remains compacted around the same central axis. Day, dining and night furniture configurations — including all 8 sleeping positions — may not obstruct this corridor or eliminate emergency circulation.

### E4. Guest/staff separation
Wet/dirty/service tasks should remain toward service/stern zones. Hosted staff are optional, not permanent occupants. The Dirty Deck is not designed as permanent crew accommodation.

### E5. Day/night transformation
Day: river-facing lounge/social space.
Night: eight single sleeping positions using OUTLAND BLOCKS, with privacy/blackout/weather/thermal protection.

### E6. Emergency access
Safe egress and operator/emergency access must be mechanically possible independent of Passport/Mystery/cloud.

## F. Boat and activity system
RAFTER carries two symmetric vertical stern racks, each with one SUP, one kayak and one tandem canoe in the defined vertical order. Final equipment quantity/capacity remains subject to load/stability verification.

Guest water activities may include SUP, kayaking and canoeing, subject to weather, current, water level, guest briefing and verified safety rules. Swimming is not assumed universally safe or lawful; conditions are `TO VERIFY`.

Required supporting systems include PFDs, paddles, rescue/first-aid equipment, secure tie-down, dry valuables storage and wet-equipment drying. Exact specifications are `TO VERIFY`.

## G. OUTLAND BLOCKS furniture
The RAFTER furniture system uses **8 single modular units** that support both lounge/day and sleeping/night configurations.

Confirmed construction concept:
- removable backrests;
- external L element behind the back;
- sewn pocket/sleeve in base and backrest receives the L element;
- no hidden docking hardware;
- no drilling through foam;
- internal/removable covers;
- 8 additional waterproof covers for post-swim/day-lounger use.

Final dimensions, foam, structural section, fabrics, storage and supplier cost require a physical prototype and quotation. Specialized detail belongs in the spatial brief and final BLOCKS supplier specification; this Master does not duplicate every fabrication detail.

## H. Guest experience
### Guest flow
1. Book via direct and/or third-party channel.
2. Receive pre-arrival and safety information.
3. Arrive at authorized berth.
4. Use Passport/credential for check-in/access.
5. Complete mandatory safety onboarding.
6. Use RAFTER independently in preferred uncrewed mode where permitted.
7. Enjoy river activities, lounge/food and optional Mystery.
8. Convert the space into night mode.
9. Sleep in up to eight single design positions.
10. Morning reset/check-out.
11. Temporary access expires.
12. `ČUVAR REKE` achievement remains if earned.

### Operator flow
Operator prepares/cleans the asset, verifies weather and equipment, activates access, resets Mystery, remains remotely available, coordinates local response and performs post-stay turnover.

## I. RAFTER Mystery Experience V1
### Theme
A quiet Cartographer layer reveals a different way of reading the river. Mystery supports observation and exploration; RAFTER does not become a classic escape room.

### Chapters
`TOK → GLAS → OBALA → NOĆ`.

Exact clue content and artifact mechanics are still `OPEN` until final spatial design and playtesting.

### Finale
A **visible but locked Cartographer's cabinet** opens after the required progression. There is no secret room. The cabinet has mechanical service override and cannot control safe circulation.

### Achievement
Completion grants `RIVER_KEEPER / ČUVAR REKE` permanently to the guest identity.

### Failure behavior
If Mystery technology fails, the guest still has a complete stay. Local/manual reset and graceful degradation are required.

### V1 scope
Two functional Passport terminals, minimal progression, light/audio feedback, cabinet actuator and persistent achievement. General game engine, distributed checkpoint network and Field Journal are deferred.
