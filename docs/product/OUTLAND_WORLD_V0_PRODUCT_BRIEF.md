# OUTLAND World V0 — Product Brief

**Status:** Gate 1 approved; implementation not yet authorized  
**Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Product:** OUTLAND World  
**First World:** RAFTER  
**Access:** Private by default

## 1. Purpose

OUTLAND World V0 is the first visitor-facing digital experience for OUTLAND.

It answers one question:

> Do I want to enter this world?

It is not an investor presentation, technical explanation or slideshow.

## 2. Audience and test

The first visitor is a 13-year-old tester using a URL and access key without live product explanation.

Observe:

- first clicks and exploration path;
- what attracts attention;
- what is skipped or unclear;
- whether RAFTER, Passport and the locked Mystery create curiosity;
- what the visitor believes OUTLAND is;
- what they want to exist in OUTLAND.

The first test is observed live. No analytics, session replay or persistent feedback collection is included.

## 3. Visitor promise

For the visitor, the product is simply:

**OUTLAND**

OUTLAND OS, COMPASS, schemas, NFC implementation and operational systems remain below the surface.

## 3.1 Thin visitor-layer boundary

OUTLAND World is a **presentation, onboarding and visitor-interaction layer**. It is not a new OUTLAND OS domain and does not own domain truth.

As OUTLAND OS capabilities are implemented, domain truth remains with the responsible capability:

- BOOKING owns reservation and inventory truth;
- PASSPORT owns persistent guest journey and selected cross-session consequences;
- UNIVERSE ENGINE owns Mystery and narrative/game state;
- SENSE owns physical devices, telemetry and commands;
- other future capabilities retain the responsibilities defined by the Platform Master Plan.

World may present and interact with these capabilities through the visitor experience, but must not create substitute implementations merely to make the visitor experience feel complete.

For V0, capabilities that do not yet exist may be represented only through curated presentation or non-persistent UX simulation where required.

**World must remain thin. New domain logic, persistence or infrastructure requires the normal OUTLAND OS use-case and build-trigger process.**

## 4. Canonical content boundary

RAFTER is the canonical first World.

`rafter` is its future canonical technical slug/code.

`RIVER_KEEPER` / `ČUVAR REKE` is an achievement/title within RAFTER, not a World.

Only facts approved in canonical RAFTER and Passport sources may appear. V0 must not reveal internal scouting, finance, property-development, safety, hardware or Mystery walkthrough information.

## 5. Experience flow

```text
ACCESS RESTRICTED
→ ENTER KEY
→ KEY ACCEPTED
→ ENTER OUTLAND
→ EXPLORE
→ RAFTER
→ PASSPORT
→ MYSTERY #001 — LOCKED
→ YOU'RE EARLY
```

## 6. V0 content scope

Include:

- private access gate;
- visual OUTLAND entry moment;
- mobile-first visitor shell;
- RAFTER as the primary explorable World;
- a few curated RAFTER discovery points;
- Passport teaser: an explorer-owned object that can unlock and remember;
- one curiosity-first Mystery state: `MYSTERY #001 — LOCKED`;
- minimal future-World / coming-later cards;
- final early-explorer screen with `What should exist in OUTLAND?`

Do not persist the final answer in V0.

## 7. UX direction

The experience should be colorful, adventurous, playful, mysterious, nature-driven and premium without feeling corporate.

Use image, motion, interaction and discovery before explanatory text. Avoid luxury-resort beige, dark-fantasy heaviness, business language and technical implementation language.

Mystery must invite curiosity without explaining its rules or revealing its path.

## 8. Explicit exclusions

- booking;
- user accounts;
- Passport backend or profile;
- NFC implementation;
- Mystery engine or progress;
- persistent guest journey;
- Supabase schema or feedback table;
- CMS;
- analytics and session replay;
- loyalty, social or gamification platform;
- public launch or `outland.one` launch.

## 9. Success criteria

The V0 test succeeds when the visitor can independently enter, navigate and describe OUTLAND as a place or universe they would like to explore.

Evidence of success includes voluntary exploration, curiosity about RAFTER/Passport/Mystery, and concrete ideas about what should exist next.

## 10. Expansion trigger

Only expand after the observed test identifies a specific validated need: richer RAFTER content, a second World, persistent feedback, Passport continuity or a real Mystery loop.
