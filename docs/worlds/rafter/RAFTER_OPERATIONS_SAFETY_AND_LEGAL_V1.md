# RAFTER OPERATIONS, SAFETY AND LEGAL V1

> **Alignment note — 2026-09-07:** Updated to incorporate owner-approved Floor Plan V2 decisions. Where older V1 wording conflicted, the 2026-09-07 decisions supersede it.

## 1. Operating principle
Preferred V1 model is **uncrewed/self-service guest stay**, subject to explicit legal, maritime, insurance and safety verification. Uncrewed means no staff permanently present during the stay; it does **not** mean unmanaged.

## 2. Guest capacity
- Design capacity: up to 8 guests / 8 single sleeping positions — `DECIDED`.
- Registered/navigation/commercial capacity: `TO VERIFY`.

## 3. Crew model
### Base case
- No permanently present crew during the stay if verified lawful and safe.
- Remote operator/owner function.
- Defined local emergency/maintenance response capability.

### Optional hosted mode
A host/cook may be present for selected packages or events. This is not the base operating requirement.

### Skipper/driver
Used when the vessel moves, transports guests, regulation requires it, or the operating plan otherwise demands qualified navigation. Minimum legal crew by scenario is `TO VERIFY`.

## 4. Turnover and housekeeping
Required operating functions:
- post-stay inspection;
- cleaning and sanitation;
- laundering/replacing internal BLOCKS covers as required;
- cleaning/drying waterproof covers;
- drying wet activity equipment;
- checking boat rack tie-downs;
- consumables replenishment;
- water and wastewater service;
- safety equipment inspection;
- Mystery reset;
- Passport/access reset;
- automatic main-entrance function and manual/emergency-release check;
- damage log and maintenance escalation.

Exact turnover time and staffing: `TO VERIFY` through pilot.

## 5. Remote operations
Because the owner may not be physically present in Serbia, RAFTER requires a delegated local operating model.

Minimum capability:
- named local responsible person/provider;
- emergency contact visible to guests;
- remote awareness of critical technical status where useful;
- ability to reach RAFTER physically within a defined SLA (`TO VERIFY`);
- access to mechanical override/service keys;
- authority to stop a stay for weather/safety reasons;
- incident documentation.

Remote monitoring should focus on justified operational signals, not surveillance of guests. Camera use inside guest/private areas is not part of the concept.

## 6. Security and anti-theft
Required risk areas:
- boat/board theft;
- outboard/propulsion theft;
- Passport credential loss;
- vandalism while unoccupied;
- unauthorized boarding;
- theft of electronics/Mystery artifacts.

Mitigations may include supervised berth, physical locks, rack locks, tamper-resistant enclosures, access logs and local response. Exact solution: `TO VERIFY`.

## 7. Seasonal / winter operations
Winterization plan is required before year-round asset operation. It must address water systems, batteries, plumbing, HVAC, fabrics, electronics, freezing, moisture and berth conditions. Whether RAFTER operates commercially in winter is `OPEN / TO VERIFY` based on economics and technical performance.

# LEGAL AND SAFETY BOUNDARY

## 8. What is known
- Target geography is Novi Sad / Danube area.
- A **legal and supervised berth** is a launch prerequisite.
- Preferred stay model is uncrewed, but this is not yet proven legally.
- Normal guest entry uses Passport/NFC-controlled automatic main entrance from Front Deck to River Room. Safe emergency exit and essential accommodation must remain independent of Passport/NFC, Mystery, network/cloud and normal powered door automation.

## 9. What is assumed
- A compact catamaran configuration can be designed to support the program.
- Commercial overnight use may be possible under an appropriate legal/registration setup.
- A supervised berth can provide enough security/support for uncrewed operations.

These assumptions are not legal conclusions.

## 10. What must be professionally verified
### Berth
- right to berth;
- commercial-use permission;
- guest access hours;
- utilities;
- security/monitoring;
- emergency vehicle/service access;
- winter conditions.

### Vessel status
- registration/classification;
- navigation zone;
- inspection obligations;
- permitted guest/passenger capacity;
- skipper/crew requirements;
- rules for a moored overnight commercial stay.

### Tourism/accommodation
- legal form of commercial lodging/experience;
- guest registration/reporting;
- taxes/fees;
- sanitary requirements;
- whether third-party booking listings are compatible with the legal setup.

### Guest transport
If RAFTER transports guests rather than remaining moored, requirements may differ materially. This must be separately verified.

### Insurance
At minimum investigate:
- hull/property;
- third-party liability;
- guest injury;
- water activities;
- theft/vandalism;
- commercial accommodation/experience;
- uncrewed occupancy exclusions.

## 11. Life safety requirements — outcome, not specification
The final professional design must provide:
- adequate life jackets/PFDs;
- rescue/recovery equipment;
- fire detection/extinguishing;
- first aid;
- safe electrical protection;
- emergency lighting as required;
- unobstructed emergency egress in day and night configurations;
- mechanical backup access/exit;
- safe boarding/disembarkation;
- weather/current/water-level operating limits;
- documented emergency procedure.

Exact quantities and standards are `TO VERIFY`.

## 12. Water and sanitation
No untreated black/grey wastewater solution should be assumed acceptable. Fresh water, toilet/shower, wastewater storage and lawful disposal require a complete operational chain and external verification before commercial launch.

## 13. Activity safety
SUP/kayak/canoe access is conditional on:
- appropriate weather/current/water-level conditions;
- guest briefing;
- required PFDs and equipment;
- age/ability rules (`TO VERIFY`);
- clear no-go conditions;
- safe launching/recovery from RAFTER or berth.

## 14. Safety independence from OUTLAND OS
The following must not rely solely on software/NFC/network:
- emergency exit;
- fire response;
- physical escape from guest areas;
- access by emergency/local operator;
- basic lighting needed for safe evacuation;
- life-saving equipment.

OUTLAND OS may enhance awareness and convenience, not become a single point of failure for life safety.
