# SIDEWORLD — Canonical Universe & Franchise Architecture V3.1

**Project Decision Summary | October 2026**

**Purpose:** Consolidate SIDEWORLD's universe strategy, narrative architecture, gameplay model, and previously agreed decisions into one reference document.

**Status:** Working canonical architecture. Product decisions are distinguished from conceptual proposals and unverified implementation details.

---

## 1. SIDEWORLD Vision

SIDEWORLD is an independent consumer platform that transforms real-world locations into playable adventures across multiple fictional and experiential universes.

The platform combines:

- Location-based city quests and puzzles
- Narrative exploration and persistent storylines
- Real-world outdoor activities and expeditions
- Discovery and collectible mechanics
- Shared player identity and progression
- AI-generated, geographically grounded content
- Physical experiences, partners, and accommodation bases
- Future cross-media franchises and licensed intellectual property

### Brand architecture

- **SIDEWORLD:** Independent consumer platform and application.
- **SIGNAL:** Shared gameplay trigger and recurring narrative mechanism.
- **The Parallel:** Potential deeper narrative concept connecting different realities.
- **Universe:** Independent canonical reality or experiential identity within SIDEWORLD.
- **OUTLAND:** One universe on the platform, not its parent brand.

**Tagline:** FOLLOW THE SIGNAL.

SIDEWORLD should become a platform for adventures across many universes rather than a single game with different thematic skins.

## 2. Approved Initial Universe Portfolio

The initial universe portfolio contains five selected universes.

This supersedes earlier brainstorming proposals suggesting ARCANA, FRACTURE, or SHADOW NETWORK as initial launch universes.

### 01. OUTLAND

**Positioning:** Real-world adventure, nature, exploration, and independence.

OUTLAND focuses on physical experiences involving natural environments, outdoor exploration, expeditions, and destination-based adventures.

**Existing world concepts:**

- GREENHILL
- RAFTER
- ORIGIN

**Activities:** Hiking, kayaking, camping, diving, snorkeling, climbing, cycling, and exploration.

**Unique characteristics:**

- Physical worlds and destination experiences
- Accommodation and expedition bases
- Outdoor equipment and partner services
- Real-world discovery and progression
- Potential land-investment intelligence based on demand versus physical capacity

OUTLAND remains a completely independent universe within SIDEWORLD.

### 02. THE UNCHARTED

**Positioning:** Lost history, exploration, hidden knowledge, and global mysteries.

THE UNCHARTED is a narrative-driven universe centered on forgotten civilizations, mysterious historical connections, missing maps, and discoveries hidden in real-world cities.

**Primary franchise:** Beyond the Atlas.

**Proposed narrative series:**

- The Lost Cartographers
- Timekeepers

**Proposed world concepts:**

- The Lost Meridian
- The Sunken Kingdoms
- The Forgotten Routes

**Activities:** Walking, historical exploration, observation, decoding, diving, snorkeling, and expedition-based discovery.

**Unique characteristics:**

- Connected international storylines
- Recurring characters and organizations
- Persistent discoveries across cities
- Long-term franchise development
- Transmedia expansion into games, books, cards, and other formats

### 03. HEARTLINES

**Positioning:** Couples, relationships, romance, and shared experiences.

HEARTLINES is a universe dedicated to meaningful adventures for couples.

Players explore cities through interactive stories, romantic mysteries, shared choices, and cooperative challenges.

**Possible experiences:**

- Romantic city discoveries
- Cooperative mystery adventures
- Relationship-oriented puzzles
- Anniversary and special-occasion quests
- Personalized couple adventures
- Shared memories and collectible moments

**Distinctive mechanics:**

- Two-player cooperation
- Optional private answers and choices
- Personalized narrative branches
- Joint achievements and memories

**Primary audience:** Couples, romantic travelers, and partners seeking memorable shared experiences.

The universe should remain accessible and entertaining rather than becoming a relationship-advice application.

### 04. WONDERLANDS

**Positioning:** Family exploration, imagination, and playful adventures.

WONDERLANDS focuses on parents, children, and multigenerational groups discovering cities together through imaginative storytelling.

**Possible experiences:**

- Magical urban treasure hunts
- Playful city exploration
- Educational discoveries
- Cooperative family puzzles
- Fantasy-inspired adventures
- Collectible characters and achievements

**Distinctive mechanics:**

- Age-adjusted difficulty
- Family cooperation
- Parent-controlled experiences
- Shorter, safer routes
- Child-friendly stories and navigation

**Primary audience:** Families with children, schools, and family-oriented tourism providers.

### 05. TASTE

**Positioning:** Food, gastronomy, local culture, and culinary exploration.

TASTE transforms cities into interactive culinary adventures.

Players discover local food traditions, markets, bakeries, restaurants, regional dishes, and cultural stories through location-based missions.

**Possible experiences:**

- Traditional food discovery quests
- Local market adventures
- Culinary mystery trails
- Neighborhood tasting experiences
- Regional ingredient hunts
- Cultural and historical food stories

**Distinctive mechanics:**

- Food discoveries and collections
- Optional tasting challenges
- Local business integration
- Culinary achievements
- Partner-supported experiences
- Dietary preference handling

**Primary audience:** Travelers, food enthusiasts, couples, and local explorers.

Partner participation and food purchases should not be mandatory for every quest.

## 3. Portfolio Status

The five selected universes are:

1. OUTLAND
2. THE UNCHARTED
3. HEARTLINES
4. WONDERLANDS
5. TASTE

These constitute the initial product portfolio.

They do not all need to launch simultaneously.

### Future universe candidates

- **ARCANA** — Urban fantasy and hidden magic
- **FRACTURE** — Parallel realities and anomalies
- **SHADOW NETWORK** — Espionage and detective adventures
- **ETERNALS** — Mythology and ancient legends
- **TOMORROW** — Optimistic science fiction and future cities

These remain exploratory concepts, not part of the initial approved portfolio.

Universe names and commercial rights require separate verification before public branding.

---

## 4. Beyond the Atlas — Confirmed Architectural Placement

The selected structure is:

**SIDEWORLD → THE UNCHARTED → Beyond the Atlas**

Specifically:

- **Platform:** SIDEWORLD
- **Universe:** THE UNCHARTED
- **Franchise:** Beyond the Atlas
- **Series:** The Lost Cartographers, Timekeepers *(proposed series structure)*
- **Worlds:** The Lost Meridian, The Sunken Kingdoms, The Forgotten Routes *(working candidates)*
- **Quests:** Individual playable adventures across real-world cities

Beyond the Atlas is **not** an independent universe and does **not** belong to OUTLAND.

---

## 5. Decision / Proposal Boundary

### Approved product decisions

- SIDEWORLD is the independent platform.
- OUTLAND is one Universe among many.
- The initial Universe portfolio is OUTLAND, THE UNCHARTED, HEARTLINES, WONDERLANDS, and TASTE.
- Beyond the Atlas belongs to THE UNCHARTED as its primary Franchise.
- SIGNAL is a shared gameplay/narrative mechanism.
- The Parallel remains a deeper narrative concept, not the platform or Universe name.

### Working / proposed creative structure

- The Lost Cartographers and Timekeepers are proposed series within Beyond the Atlas.
- The Lost Meridian, The Sunken Kingdoms, and The Forgotten Routes are working World candidates.
- Future Universe candidates remain exploratory until separately approved.

### Unverified commercial / implementation details

- Universe names and commercial rights require separate verification before public branding.
- This document defines product architecture; it does not assert that every approved Universe or Franchise has already been seeded into production data.
