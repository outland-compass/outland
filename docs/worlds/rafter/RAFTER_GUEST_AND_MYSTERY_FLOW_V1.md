# RAFTER GUEST AND MYSTERY FLOW V1

## 1. Product stance
RAFTER is a curated river experience / mini-expedition that may include overnight accommodation. Mystery is integral to the identity but optional to complete. The river, autonomy, exploration and transformable space remain valuable even for a guest who ignores the Mystery.

## 2. Guest lifecycle
### 2.1 Discovery and booking
Guest discovers RAFTER through direct OUTLAND channels and/or third-party booking channels. V1 does not require a custom OUTLAND booking engine. Reservation data must nevertheless be capable of creating a bounded guest access lifecycle.

### 2.2 Pre-arrival
Guest receives:
- location/arrival instructions;
- what to bring;
- water-activity limitations;
- safety expectations;
- check-in window;
- contact/escalation method;
- Passport setup/credential instructions as applicable.

### 2.3 Arrival and check-in
Preferred V1 mode supports self-service/uncrewed arrival where legally and operationally permitted.

Flow:
1. Guest reaches the authorized berth/access point.
2. Passport/credential is presented at Entrance Terminal.
3. Valid reservation/access window is checked locally or via resilient service.
4. Access is granted.
5. Arrival event is recorded.
6. Guest receives mandatory safety onboarding before unrestricted use.
7. Mystery may become available, but safe accommodation does not depend on it.

### 2.4 Safety onboarding
Must cover at minimum, subject to professional verification:
- emergency exits;
- fire equipment;
- life jackets;
- river-activity rules;
- weather/current/water-level restrictions;
- how to contact local assistance;
- what happens if power/network/Passport fails;
- use of wet areas and equipment.

### 2.5 Day experience
Core experiences may include:
- river-facing living;
- SUP, kayak and tandem canoe use where conditions permit;
- swimming only where legally/safely permitted (`TO VERIFY`);
- relaxing and social use of OUTLAND BLOCKS;
- food preparation / simple hosted food options;
- exploration of RAFTER Mystery cues.

### 2.6 Evening transformation
Guest or operator converts the guest zone from day/lounge configuration into night configuration using 8 OUTLAND BLOCKS single modules. Conversion must be understandable, fast and safe without specialist tools. Exact SOP follows physical prototype.

### 2.7 Overnight
Night mode provides protected sleeping positions, privacy, blackout/light control, thermal comfort and safe emergency egress. Mystery cannot lock guests into or out of essential spaces.

### 2.8 Morning and checkout
- Return BLOCKS toward day mode or follow checkout SOP.
- Return and secure activity equipment.
- Report damage/incident if any.
- Access window ends after checkout.
- Temporary accommodation access is revoked.
- Persistent achievement remains in Passport profile if earned.

## 3. Operator lifecycle
Separate from guest experience:
1. Confirm reservation and access window.
2. Inspect weather/water-level restrictions.
3. Prepare/clean RAFTER.
4. Reset Mystery state safely.
5. Verify PFDs, fire equipment and required inventory.
6. Issue/activate Passport credential.
7. Enable local access.
8. Remotely monitor defined safety/technical signals during uncrewed stay.
9. Provide local response contact.
10. Revoke access at checkout and perform turnover inspection.

Exact automation is not a V1 requirement; manual operations are acceptable where safe and reliable.

# RAFTER MYSTERY EXPERIENCE V1

## 4. Tone and design rule
Mystery should feel like discovering a hidden layer of the river journey, not entering a themed escape room. It should be quiet, atmospheric, tactile and connected to observation of water, sound, bank and night.

## 5. Cartographer premise
A recurring Cartographer figure has left a sequence of observations, signals and physical traces. Guests who choose to follow them gradually interpret the river through four chapters and eventually earn access to the Cartographer's visible locked cabinet.

The exact prose, puzzle mechanics and artifact design remain `OPEN` until aligned with the final physical space.

## 6. Chapter structure
### `TOK` — Flow
Purpose: notice movement/current/direction and begin the Mystery. Physical/observational clue: `OPEN`. Desired reaction may include subtle light/audio confirmation.

### `GLAS` — Voice
Purpose: use sound/listening as the next layer. Audio artifact/trigger: `OPEN`. Avoid loud attraction-style effects.

### `OBALA` — Bank
Purpose: direct attention beyond the vessel toward the riverbank/environment. Exact checkpoint and whether it requires leaving RAFTER: `OPEN / TO VERIFY` for safety.

### `NOĆ` — Night
Purpose: final atmospheric chapter using darkness, orientation, light or sound. Must never require unsafe behavior near water at night. Exact mechanic: `OPEN`.

## 7. Finale — Cartographer's cabinet
- Cabinet is physically visible in the room/guest environment.
- It is locked until the required Mystery state is satisfied.
- It is not a hidden room and does not conceal a secret space large enough for entry.
- Opening may be actuated locally by the Mystery terminal/SENSE controller.
- Mechanical service override is required.
- Failure to open never traps or blocks a guest.

Contents/reward artifact: `OPEN`.

## 8. Achievement
Successful V1 completion grants:
- technical code: `RIVER_KEEPER`;
- guest-facing name: `ČUVAR REKE`.

Achievement is persistent after accommodation access expires.

## 9. Passport touchpoints
### Prototype Terminal 1 — Entrance
Minimum responsibility:
- recognize credential;
- validate temporary access;
- permit/deny entry;
- start arrival/Mystery context;
- record event when connectivity allows;
- function with defined offline fallback.

### Prototype Terminal 2 — Mystery
Minimum responsibility:
- recognize credential;
- read local/current Mystery progression;
- accept/deny a valid checkpoint;
- trigger local light/audio response;
- actuate cabinet when final state permits;
- queue/sync event when online connectivity returns.

Additional checkpoints are `DEFERRED` until V1 playtesting proves they are needed.

## 10. Safe reset
Between stays the operator must be able to:
- return cabinet to locked start state;
- clear temporary Mystery progression for the reservation/party;
- preserve earned persistent achievement in the guest's identity;
- test terminal/actuator operation;
- bypass/disable Mystery without disabling accommodation.

## 11. Technology failure mode
If internet fails:
- validated temporary access should continue within a defined local/offline policy;
- queued events may sync later;
- Mystery may continue locally if state is available;
- if Mystery fails, guests still have normal stay and safe exit.

If power/controller/lock fails:
- safe exit remains mechanical/independent;
- operator/local responder has service access;
- Mystery degrades to non-interactive clues rather than creating a safety problem.

## 12. Pilot vs later
### V1 pilot
- two functional Passport terminals;
- minimal four-chapter state progression;
- local light/audio responses;
- one cabinet actuator;
- persistent achievement;
- manual operator reset supported.
